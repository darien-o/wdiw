How it works

From a title to the right app in four steps.

Where Do I Watch does not stream anything. It finds where a title is legally available and hands you to the app you already have.

1

Search

Type with the remote or just say the title. It also answers the TV's own launcher search.

2

Look it up

A small proxy asks TMDB where the title streams in your country, and Wikidata for its ID on each platform.

3

Match your TV

The app checks which of those streaming apps are installed and puts the best option first.

4

Open it there

Press OK to jump to the title, or to the app's search. Not installed? Scan a QR code on your phone.

Under the hood

Small, open and easy to fix.

Three parts. The rules for opening each streaming app are plain data, so fixing a link never needs an app update.

android-tv/

TV app

Kotlin and Compose for TV. Opens the provider's app with an intent.

proxy/

Cloudflare Worker

Keeps the TMDB key off devices, caches answers, rate-limits and serves the registry.

TMDB + JustWatch

What the title is and where it streams.

Wikidata

The title's ID on each platform.

No scraping

Availability comes from TMDB, platform IDs from Wikidata. No unofficial APIs.

Nothing about you

No analytics, no account. The app keeps no record of you or what you search.

Small and fast

A single APK under 6 MB that starts quickly, even on a 1 GB projector.

Providers

Contribute

Got a TV? You can help today.

The most useful contribution is checking that a streaming app's links open the right screen on your TV. No Kotlin needed.

📺

Test a provider

Run one adb command, note what opens, and add a row to the verification log.

 🔎

Report a wrong result

Open an issue with the title, your country and what the app showed.

 🌍

Translate

Copy one strings.xml file into your language. English and Spanish are done.

 🛠️

Write code

Read the architecture notes, then pick an issue. Decision logic is plain Kotlin with unit tests.

 
# Does a link open the title on your TV?
$ adb shell am start -a android.intent.action.VIEW \
    -d "https://app.primevideo.com/detail?gti=…" \
    com.amazon.amazonvideo.livingroom

# Check the registry and run the tests
$ cd proxy && pnpm run check:registry && pnpm test
$ cd android-tv && ./gradlew testDebugUnitTest

# Drive the app from outside
$ adb shell am start -a android.intent.action.VIEW \
    -d "wdiw://search?q=inception"
